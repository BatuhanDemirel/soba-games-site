This privacy policy explains how the Kahvehane mobile application (the "App") handles user data. The App is developed by Batuhan Demirel.

1. WE DO NOT COLLECT PERSONAL DATA

The App does not collect, store or transmit personal information such as your name, e-mail address, phone number or location. It does not require you to create an account or sign in. The App has no server of its own.

2. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The App stores the following information on your device only; none of it is sent anywhere:

  • Game saves and matches in progress
  • Career points, rank and statistics
  • Language, sound and display preferences
  • Records of recent matches, used by the evaluation feature

When you uninstall the App, this data is deleted from your device.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The App uses two Google services:

Google AdMob (serving ads): may process data such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type, ad interactions and diagnostic/crash logs in order to serve and measure ads. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play Billing (in-app purchases): purchase status information is processed in order to verify the "Remove Ads" purchase. Your card details are handled by Google; the App has no access to them.

4. YOUR AD CHOICES

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads).

5. USERS IN THE EUROPEAN UNION

Users located in the EU/EEA are shown a consent screen the first time the App is opened. If you do not accept personalised ads, non-personalised ads are shown instead.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. INTERNET USE

Your game data is never sent to any server. An internet connection is used only for serving ads and for purchase transactions.

8. DELETING YOUR DATA

You can delete the data held on your device by uninstalling the App. For advertising data processed by Google, you can use Google's privacy tools.

9. CHANGES

This policy may be updated. Any changes will be published on this page.

10. CONTACT

For questions: kahvehanedestek@gmail.com
